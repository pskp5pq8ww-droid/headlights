# Shining Headlights Australia - Special Offer Meta Ads Campaign

## Campaign Goal

Generate bookings for the special limited-time mobile headlight restoration offer in Brisbane.

Primary offer:
- Special Limited-Time Headlight Restoration Offer
- From $99
- Was $220
- Save $121
- Limited time only
- We come to your home, workplace or driveway across Brisbane

Primary CTA:
- Claim Special Offer

Landing page:
- `/special-offer`

## Core Audiences

Cold local audience:
- Brisbane and surrounding areas
- Car owners, commuters, families, tradies, used car owners
- Pain points: cloudy/yellow headlights, car looks older, poor night confidence, replacement cost

Warm retargeting:
- Website visitors
- Booking page visitors who did not submit
- People who engaged with Facebook/Instagram profile or ads
- Video viewers from before/after or process content

## Compliance Notes

- Avoid implying guaranteed safety outcomes.
- Use "helps improve", "better night confidence", and "cleaner-looking headlights" instead of absolute claims.
- Add "from $99" and "final price may vary" on landing page or caption when relevant.
- Keep text large enough for mobile feed and story placements.
